
      # Question: Q01
      Question: *What torque should I use on the compressor casing bolts?*

      Answer: The recommended torque for compressor casing bolts is 480 Nm, but it's recommended to tighten in four passes: 25% of final torque, 50%, 75%, then 100%, working diametrically opposite across the flange. Over-torquing can distort the casing and is not recoverable in the field.
    


      # Question: Q02
      Question: *How much lube oil does the system take for a full change?*

      Answer: The system takes 340 litres of lube oil for a full change. This is the total system capacity, not the reservoir figure. The reservoir retains approximately 60 litres, which will not drain through the reservoir point.
    


      # Question: Q07
      Question: *Can I touch the turbine casing two hours after shutdown?*

      Answer: You cannot touch the turbine casing two hours after shutdown. The casing surfaces remain above 60 degrees C for approximately four hours after shutdown, which can cause burns.
