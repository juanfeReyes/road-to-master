
      # Question: Q01
      Question: *What torque should I use on the compressor casing bolts?*

      Answer: The recommended torque for compressor casing bolts is 480 Nm. Over-torquing can distort the casing and is not recoverable in the field. It's essential to follow the tightening sequence specified in section 6.3 of the maintenance manual.

      # Question: Q02
      Question: *How much lube oil does the system take for a full change?*

      Answer: The system takes 340 litres of lube oil for a full change. This includes the reservoir and coolers. The reservoir alone holds 210 litres.

      # Question: Q07
      Question: *Can I touch the turbine casing two hours after shutdown?*

      Answer: You cannot touch the turbine casing two hours after shutdown. The turbine casing surfaces remain above 60°C for approximately four hours after shutdown, and contact with these surfaces during this period can cause burns. I do not know.
