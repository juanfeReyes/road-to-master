
      # Question: Q01
      Question: *What torque should I use on the compressor casing bolts?*

      Answer: The recommended torque for the compressor casing bolts is 480 Nm. Tightening should be done in four passes, with the final pass completed within 30 minutes of the third pass.
    


      # Question: Q02
      Question: *How much lube oil does the system take for a full change?*

      Answer: The system takes a total capacity of 420 litres for a full lube oil change, including the reservoir and coolers. The reservoir alone holds 210 litres, and filling to this figure would leave the system underfilled. The total system capacity is given in section 4 of the manual.
