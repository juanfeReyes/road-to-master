# HT-400 Quick Reference Card - Combustion Liner Change (Synthetic)

> Laminated card for field use. Not a substitute for the maintenance manual.

## Before you start

Confirm shutdown and isolation. Check the manual for general safety requirements.

## Steps

1. Remove combustion casing cover bolts.
2. Support the liner.
3. Remove final fixings and withdraw the liner squarely.
4. Inspect the transition piece seal.
5. Fit the new liner with new bolts.
6. Torque to 95 Nm.
7. Refit the cover.

## Do not

- Do not reuse combustion liner bolts.
- Do not lever against the transition piece.
