# Halcyon Turbine Systems - Service Bulletin SB-2026-04 (Synthetic)

> Issued 3 March 2026. Applies to HT-400 units with serial numbers below HT400-2200.
> Read in conjunction with the HT-400 Field Maintenance Manual.

## Subject

Inlet guide vane pivot nut - revised torque and inspection requirement.

## Background

Field reports have identified pivot nut loosening on units operating with frequent
load changes. Investigation found the existing torque value insufficient for
high-cycle duty.

## Action required

For affected units, the inlet guide vane pivot nut torque is revised **from 22 Nm to
28 Nm**. This supersedes the value in section 3 of the maintenance manual for
affected units only. Units at or above serial HT400-2200 retain the 22 Nm value.

Additionally, inspect the pivot nut for witness-mark movement at every 500 hour
interval, not only at linkage adjustment.

## Note for maintenance planners

This bulletin does not change any other torque value, and does not change the
scheduled maintenance interval structure.
